## ARX LIFT2

- Path: `rsi/lift2_suite/common/models/lift2/` - `source_lift2_dual_arm.urdf` (as received), `patched.urdf`,
  `raw_from_urdf.xml`, `scene*.xml`, `meshes/*.STL` (25 meshes) and `meshes/finger_cd/*.STL` (convex
  decompositions of the four finger links made by us), plus our notes `PATCHES.txt`, `V1_NOTES.txt`,
  `finger_cd_*.json`, `scene_params.json`.
- Origin: a single-URDF package of the ARX LIFT2 dual-arm platform (two arms, platform, grippers, 45-degree head
  bracket, three cameras). Its comments say the platform link was cut from the original LIFT2 `body.STEP`, that the
  wrist camera bracket is the ARX official D405 bracket, and that the gripper was re-modelled with one opening DoF per
  hand. Our changes are listed in `PATCHES.txt` / `V1_NOTES.txt` (compiler tag, gripper joint damping/armature,
  finger collision meshes replaced by 12 convex hulls each).
- Licence: **not stated** - no licence file or copyright notice is included in the package. The meshes are derived
  from ARX vendor CAD and are included only to reproduce the evaluation.
- `d405_camera.STL` and `d435_camera.STL` look like Intel RealSense camera models; their licence is unknown.

## Dual-arm Franka

- Path: `rsi/franka_suite/model/` - `meshes/franka1_tilted_mounting_flat.urdf` (xacro output, "franka1"),
  `meshes/link0.stl` ... `link7.stl`, `hand.stl` (STL headers: "Exported from Blender-2.79", the format of the Franka
  description collision meshes), `umi_finger.stl`, `base_link.STL` (decimated by us), `Torso_Link.STL`;
  `franka_robot.xml` / `franka_planning.xml` are generated from the URDF by `build_robot.py`.
- Origin: the URDF was generated from a `franka1_description` xacro of the dual-arm station (FR3 arms, Franka Hand
  with UMI-type fingers on a tilted mount) and was provided to us with the robot; the comments reference
  `franka1_description/robots/fr3/*.yaml`.
- Licence: **not stated.**
  - FR3 links and Franka Hand: very likely from Franka Robotics' description package (`franka_description`,
    Apache-2.0). Confirm the source and keep the Apache-2.0 notice if so.
  - `umi_finger.stl`: UMI-type finger (Universal Manipulation Interface, `real-stanford/universal_manipulation_interface`,
    MIT). Confirm the source.
  - `base_link.STL`, `Torso_Link.STL`: station / mounting structure; origin and licence unknown.

## xTrainer

- Path: `robots/xtrainer/` - `meshes/*.obj` (9 visual meshes: base, links J1_1 ... J1_8), `collision_meshes/*.obj`
  (convex parts of the gripper housing and fingers made by us), `contact_parts/*.obj`, `nova2_reference.urdf`
  (kinematic reference, created by the SolidWorks-to-URDF exporter; robot name `nova2_robot`).
- Origin: X-Trainer assets, repository `https://github.com/embodied-dobot/x-trainer`, asset repository
  `https://huggingface.co/dstx123/xtrainer-leisaac` (revision `fb140bf9d4bf7de8b71818df59b93a1cb4c0564a`, URDF-imported
  USD of `x-trainer_asm-0226`); the arms are Dobot Nova 2. Meshes were exported from that USD.
- Licence: **not stated** in the x-trainer repository or the Hugging Face asset repository; the origin of
  `nova2_reference.urdf` is recorded in its header.

## Rebuilt task objects (authors' own)

All other meshes in `worlds/xtrainer/*/meshes/` (cubes, bar, drawer, cabinet shell, box, carton, dustpan, brush) were
generated by the authors (Blender parametric models and trimesh exports) from their own demonstrations, and the
reconstruction records, reference trajectories (`reference.npz`, `motor_targets.npz`, `dynamics.npz`) and layouts are
derived from the authors' own recordings. They fall under the licence of this repository.

## SAM3

Not redistributed. Users install SAM3 from `https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3` (we used commit
`0570b3a5be9c4e694f23d85232fb55f4a6f1f7fc`) and download the `sam3.pt` checkpoint from the official source after
accepting the SAM License. Results in the paper were produced with that checkpoint.
